# Cards

Cards are the fundamental units of work in Mello. Each card represents a task, idea, or item that can be enriched with details, assigned to members, and tracked through your workflow.

## Creating a Card

1. Click **Add a card** at the bottom of any list
2. Enter a title
3. Press Enter or click outside to save

Cards appear at the bottom of the list by default.

## Card Details

Click any card to open the detail modal with these sections:

### Title & Description

- **Title** - Concise name for the task
- **Description** - Rich text area for detailed information, notes, or instructions

### Members

Assign team members to the card:
- Click **Members** in the sidebar
- Select users from workspace members
- Assigned members appear on the card front
- Members receive notifications about card updates

### Labels

Categorize cards with colored labels:
- Click **Labels** to add/remove
- Labels are defined at the board level
- Multiple labels can be applied to one card

### Due Date

Set deadlines for cards:
- Click **Due Date** to open date picker
- Select date and time
- Mark as complete when done
- Overdue dates show in red

### Checklists

Break down tasks into subtasks:
- Add multiple checklists per card
- Each checklist has its own items
- Track progress with completion percentage

### Attachments

Attach files to cards:
- Upload images, documents, PDFs
- Files stored in MinIO/S3
- Preview images inline

### Comments

Collaborate through discussion:
- Add comments to discuss the card
- Edit or delete your own comments
- Members receive notifications

### Custom Fields

Add structured data:
- Fill in custom fields defined on the board
- Supports text, number, date, checkbox, dropdown

## Drag and Drop

Cards support intuitive drag-and-drop:

### Move Between Lists
- Drag card to another list
- Drop at specific position

### Reorder Within List
- Drag card up or down
- New position saves automatically

### Multi-user Sync
- Changes appear in real-time for all viewers

## Card Front

The card preview on the board shows:
- Title
- Labels (colored bars)
- Due date badge
- Member avatars
- Checklist progress
- Comment/attachment counts

## Archive & Restore

### Archive a Card
- Open card → **Archive**
- Removes from board view
- Data is preserved

### Restore a Card
- Access archived cards from board menu
- Click **Send to Board** to restore

### Delete Permanently
- Archived cards can be permanently deleted
- ⚠️ This action cannot be undone
