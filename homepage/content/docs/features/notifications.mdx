# Notifications

Notifications keep you informed about changes and activity on boards, lists, and cards you're watching or assigned to.

## Notification Types

| Type | Trigger |
|------|---------|
| Card created | New card added to watched board/list |
| Card updated | Changes to watched cards |
| Card moved | Card moved between lists |
| Card completed | Due date marked complete |
| Member assigned | You are added to a card |
| Member removed | You are removed from a card |
| Comment added | New comment on your cards |
| Due date changed | Due date set or modified |
| Label added/removed | Label changes on watched cards |

## Notification Center

Access notifications from the bell icon in the header:

### Notification List
- Shows all your notifications
- Newest first
- Click to view related card

### Unread Badge
- Red badge shows unread count
- Updates in real-time
- Clears when you view notifications

### Mark as Read
- Click a notification to mark as read
- Or use "Mark all as read" button

### Mark as Unread
- Keep important notifications visible
- Click menu → Mark as unread

## Watching

Subscribe to receive notifications by watching:

### Watch a Board

1. Open a board
2. Click **Watch** in board menu
3. Receive all board activity notifications

### Watch a List

1. Click list menu (⋮)
2. Select **Watch**
3. Receive notifications for that list

### Stop Watching

1. Click **Watching** button
2. Notifications stop for that resource

## What You're Automatically Notified About

You receive notifications when:
- You are assigned to a card
- Someone comments on your card
- Your due date approaches
- You are mentioned (if supported)

## Notification Settings

Control what notifications you receive:
- Watch/unwatch specific boards or lists
- Automatic notifications for assignments

## Real-time Delivery

Notifications are delivered instantly via WebSocket:
- Badge updates immediately
- No page refresh needed
- Works across browser tabs
