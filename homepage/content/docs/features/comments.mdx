# Comments

Comments enable team collaboration by allowing members to discuss cards, ask questions, provide updates, and share information.

## Adding Comments

1. Open a card
2. Scroll to the **Activity** section
3. Type your comment in the text box
4. Click **Save** or press Cmd/Ctrl + Enter

## Comment Features

### Text Content
- Write detailed comments
- Supports plain text

### Author & Timestamp
Each comment displays:
- Author's name and avatar
- Time posted (updates to relative time)

### Edit Comments
Edit your own comments:
1. Hover over your comment
2. Click **Edit**
3. Modify the text
4. Click **Save**

Only the comment author can edit their comments.

### Delete Comments
Remove your own comments:
1. Hover over your comment
2. Click **Delete**
3. Confirm deletion

Deleted comments cannot be recovered.

## Activity Feed

Comments appear in the card's activity feed:
- Newest comments at the top
- Mixed with other card activities
- Shows all comment history

## Notifications

Comments trigger notifications for:
- **Card members** - Users assigned to the card
- **Board watchers** - Users watching the board
- **List watchers** - Users watching the list

Notifications are delivered in real-time via WebSocket.

## Best Practices

**Effective commenting:**
- 📝 Provide context and details
- ❓ Ask clear questions
- ✅ Update on progress
- 🔗 Reference related information
- 👋 @mention team members (if supported)
