# Lists

Lists are vertical columns on a board that organize cards into workflow stages (e.g., "To Do", "In Progress", "Done").

## Creating a List

1. Click **Add another list** at the right side of the board
2. Enter a title (e.g., "To Do")
3. Press Enter or click **Add List**

## List Actions

Access these from the list menu (⋮):

### Move List
- Drag and drop to reorder lists on the board
- Position is saved automatically

### Copy List
- Duplicates the list with all its cards
- Cards are copied with titles, descriptions, and due dates
- New list is created next to the original

### Watch List
- Subscribe to notifications for this list
- Get notified when cards are added, moved, or updated

### Archive List
- Removes the list from the board view
- Can be restored from board menu
- Archived lists retain all cards

## Card Management in Lists

### Add a Card
- Click **Add a card** at the bottom of the list
- Enter title and press Enter

### Move All Cards
- Move all cards in this list to another list
- Useful for bulk reorganization

### Sort Cards
Order cards by:
- **Created date** - Newest or oldest first
- **Due date** - Soonest or latest first
- **Alphabetically** - A-Z or Z-A

### Archive All Cards
- Archive every card in the list at once
- The list remains but becomes empty

## Collapse/Expand

- Click the collapse icon to minimize a list
- Shows only the header with card count
- Click again to expand

## Drag and Drop

Lists support smooth drag-and-drop:
- Drag list header to reorder
- Changes sync in real-time
- Position updates persist automatically
